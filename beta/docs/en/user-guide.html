AI Stamp Collection Scanner

User Guide — Browser Beta v1.0

Purpose

The Browser Beta creates an editable stamp inventory in which each record remains linked to its source photo. It can also highlight stamps that may be interesting to research further.

Add photos

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported. Each upload receives its own photo number, even if filenames are the same. One inventory can contain up to 20 photos.

Create the inventory

Open the AI Stamp Scanner, select your photos and choose Create inventory. The scanner creates inventory records based on the stamps in the photo.

Review and edit

Check that all stamps in the photo are included in the inventory. Edit details when needed. If a record is linked to the wrong source photo, change the source photo.

Local storage and privacy

Photos and draft inventory data stay locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Browser storage can be cleared, so save completed work regularly as an Excel or CSV file.

Save your inventory

You can save the inventory as an Excel or CSV file. Both contain Record ID, Photo Number, Original Filename and Image Reference. Photos and thumbnails are not embedded in Excel in Browser Beta v1.0.

Limit: the Browser Beta helps with inventorying and identifying stamps that may deserve further research. It does not determine value, rarity, authenticity, watermark, exact perforation or catalogue number.
