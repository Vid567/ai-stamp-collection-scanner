Frequently Asked Questions

Does the scanner run entirely in the browser?

Yes. The Browser Beta runs locally in a modern web browser. No Python or other software installation is required.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos stay locally in your browser and are not sent to an external server.

Does the scanner automatically identify every stamp?

No. The Browser Beta mainly helps create and edit a structured stamp inventory and can indicate which stamps may be interesting to research further.

Does the scanner determine value or rarity?

No. The app does not provide an appraisal and does not make reliable claims about value, rarity, authenticity, watermark, exact perforation or catalogue number.

What happens if one photo contains multiple stamps?

The Browser Beta creates inventory records for stamps it recognises in the photo. Check afterwards that all stamps in the photo are included and edit details where needed.

Which file formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Can I save my inventory?

Yes. You can save the stamp inventory as an Excel or CSV file.

Are the photos included in the Excel file?

No. Browser Beta v1.0 stores text references only, including Record ID, Photo Number, Original Filename and Image Reference. Photos and thumbnails are not embedded in Excel.

The Browser Beta helps inventory a stamp collection and identify stamps that may deserve further research. It does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.
