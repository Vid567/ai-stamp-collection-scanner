Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about photos, privacy, detection, inventory creation and export in Browser Beta v1.0.

What kind of photo works best?

A sharp photo taken directly from above, with enough light and every stamp fully visible. Keep loose stamps from overlapping.

Can I use crowded album pages?

Yes, but review the result extra carefully. Many small stamps, text, cancellations and little space between stamps make detection harder.

Can I photograph a pile of loose stamps?

We do not recommend it. Rotated stamps may still be detected, but overlap is a bigger problem because hidden parts are not visible. Separate the stamps and take another photo.

Will the scanner always find every stamp?

No. Browser Beta tries to detect visible physical stamps, but it can miss stamps or make incorrect detections. Always review the inventory.

Does the scanner run entirely in the browser?

Yes. The Browser Beta runs locally in a modern web browser.

Does the scanner automatically identify a stamp's country?

Only if you choose to: paste your own free Google Gemini API key at the top of the scanner page, and the AI fills in country, period, value and more automatically (marked "AI suggestion", still to be checked). Without a key these fields stay "Unknown" for manual entry, as always.

How are photos, inventory data and analytics handled?

Stamp photos and inventory data are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded for stamp identification. Anonymous usage analytics may be collected to help improve the beta.

Does the scanner determine value or rarity?

No. The app does not provide an appraisal and does not determine value, rarity, authenticity, watermark, exact perforation or catalogue number.

Which file formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Can I save my inventory?

Yes. You can save the stamp inventory as an Excel or CSV file.

Are photos included in Excel?

No. Browser Beta v1.0 stores textual photo references. Photos and thumbnails are not embedded in Excel.

What happens when several stamps look identical?

Each separately visible physical stamp should produce its own record, even when stamps look identical. Quantity remains editable, but it is not used to collapse detected physical stamps.

How do later batches and photo numbers work?

An inventory accepts up to 20 photos. Photos added in later batches continue the numbering within the active browser session. Save the inventory before starting a separate session because browser storage can be cleared.

A good photo improves the chance of a complete inventory, but automatic detection remains an aid. Always review the result.
