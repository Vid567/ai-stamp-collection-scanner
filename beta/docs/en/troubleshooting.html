Troubleshooting

Some stamps are missing from the inventory

Check that all stamps are clearly visible in the photos. Then check whether all stamps are included in the inventory. If needed, take a sharper photo with better lighting.

Photos are not accepted

Use JPG/JPEG, PNG or WebP and check that you are not adding more than 20 photos to one inventory.

The inventory is not created

Make sure at least one supported photo has been added, then choose Create inventory. If nothing happens, refresh the page and try again with fewer photos.

Saving as Excel does not work

Try saving again and check whether your browser allows downloads. If Excel still does not work, save the inventory as CSV instead. Your inventory remains available in the browser unless browser data is cleared.

Saving as CSV does not work

Check whether downloads are blocked by your browser. Refresh the page if needed and try again.

The browser is slow

Close other heavy browser tabs, use fewer photos at once or refresh the page and reopen the scanner.

I refreshed or closed the browser

The Browser Beta stores draft inventory data locally in your browser where possible. Browser storage can still be cleared by browser settings, private browsing, device cleanup or manual deletion. Save completed work regularly as Excel or CSV.

I am unsure about a stamp

The Browser Beta helps inventory a stamp collection and can highlight stamps that may be interesting to research further. It does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.

I found a problem or have an idea

When reporting a problem, include what happened, what you expected, which device and browser you used, and a screenshot if possible. Also tell us what you would like to see added to the app.

Tip: Save your work regularly as an Excel or CSV file so you always have a current copy of your stamp inventory.
