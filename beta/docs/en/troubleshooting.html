Troubleshooting

Practical fixes for Browser Beta v1.0.

Check the photo first

✓ Good

Place loose stamps next to each other without overlap. Photograph straight from above, in focus and with enough light. Make sure every stamp is fully visible.

△ Difficult but usable

A busy album page, a slightly angled photo, text or postmarks may still be usable, but they make recognition harder. Check the result extra carefully.

✕ Not recommended

Loose stamps lying on top of each other. Overlap hides parts of stamps, so the scanner may miss a stamp or see two stamps as one object.

Stamps are missing from the inventory

Compare the inventory with the source photo. For missed stamps, take a new photo straight from above, in focus and with enough light. Make every stamp fully visible and place loose stamps next to each other without overlap.

Stamps overlap

Overlap is not recommended because part of the lower stamp is hidden. Place loose stamps next to each other without overlap and photograph them again.

The scanner sees two stamps as one

This can happen when stamps touch or overlap. Leave more space between loose stamps. On album pages, check that individual stamps are clearly distinguishable.

A full album page gives too few results

Photograph the page as straight as possible, use good light and keep small stamps sharp. If needed, take two or more detail photos instead of one photo from far away.

Text or postmarks are detected incorrectly

Review the detected rows and remove or correct a false detection. A calm background and clear stamp edges help.

Photos are not added

Use JPG/JPEG, PNG or WebP and no more than 100 photos per inventory.

Excel export does not work

Check that your browser allows downloads. If Excel still does not work, save the inventory as CSV.

The browser becomes slow

Close other heavy tabs, use fewer photos at once or try smaller/detail photos.

AI identification stays "Unknown"

That's normal as long as no key is entered — AI identification is optional. To use it, paste a valid free Google Gemini API key (get one at Google AI Studio, no credit card required) into the box at the top of the page. Still not working? Check that the key was copied correctly, that you haven't hit your free daily quota with Google, and that your internet connection is active.

I am unsure about the result

The Browser Beta is an aid and does not guarantee complete recognition. Always check the automatically created inventory against the source photo. The scanner does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.

Photo rule: visible + sharp + enough light + no overlap gives the scanner the best chance to find each physical stamp separately. Even with a good photo, always check the created inventory.
