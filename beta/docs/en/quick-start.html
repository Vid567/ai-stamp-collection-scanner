AI Stamp Collection Scanner

Quick Start — Browser Beta v1.0

  1. Open the AI Stamp Scanner.
  2. Choose one or more JPG/JPEG, PNG or WebP photos.
  3. Check that each photo receives a photo number.
  4. Select Create inventory.
  5. Check that all stamps in the photo are included in the inventory.
  6. Review and edit the details where needed.
  7. Save the inventory as an Excel or CSV file.

Photos stay locally in your browser. The Browser Beta helps inventory a stamp collection and highlights stamps that may be interesting to research further. It does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.
