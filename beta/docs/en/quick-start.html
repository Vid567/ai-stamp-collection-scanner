AI Stamp Collection Scanner

Quick Start — Browser Beta v1.0

Use these seven steps to create, check and save your first stamp inventory.

  1. Open the AI Stamp Scanner.
  2. Choose one or more JPG/JPEG, PNG or WebP photos.
  3. Check that each photo receives a photo number.
  4. Select Create inventory. The Beta creates one starting record for each photo.
  5. If a photo contains more than one stamp, use Add record or Duplicate so every stamp has its own record.
  6. Review and edit the details and source photo where needed.
  7. Save the inventory as an Excel or CSV file.

Browser Beta v1.0 focuses on organising, reviewing and exporting a stamp inventory. It does not automatically identify every stamp or provide a valuation.

Photos stay locally in your browser. Unknown details remain available for manual review. The Browser Beta does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.
