AI Stamp Collection Scanner

Quick Start — Browser Beta v1.0

Use these steps to create, review and save your first stamp inventory.

Take a good photo first

✓ Good

Photograph from directly above, in focus and with enough light. Keep every stamp fully visible and separate from other stamps.

△ Difficult but usable

A busy album page, a slightly angled photo, or text and cancellations may still work, but review the result extra carefully.

✕ Not recommended

A loose pile of rotated and overlapping stamps. Separate the stamps and take another photo. Hidden parts cannot be detected reliably.

  1. Open the AI Stamp Scanner.
  2. Choose one or more JPG/JPEG, PNG or WebP photos.
  3. Check that each photo receives a photo number.
  4. Select Create inventory. The scanner tries to find the visible stamps separately.
  5. Check the number of detected stamps. Add a missing record or correct a mistake when needed.
  6. Review and edit the details and source photo.
  7. Save the inventory as an Excel or CSV file.

Optional: want AI to fill in country, period and value instead of "Unknown"? Paste your own free Google Gemini API key (get one at Google AI Studio, no credit card required) into the box at the top of the scanner page first.

Important: Even with a good photo, Browser Beta may miss a stamp. Always review the created inventory before saving it.

Photos stay locally in your browser. The scanner does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.
