A simple cataloging workflow

Photograph in manageable groups

Use clear, straight-on images. Keep stamps separated where possible so edges and designs remain visible.

Create one structured inventory

Give every detected stamp a record and keep the source photo linked to that record.

Research only what matters

Add notes first. Investigate uncertain or interesting-looking records later instead of stopping on every stamp.

What to record

Core reference

Record ID, photo number and original image reference.

Visible details

Country or region, denomination, colour, subject and readable text where visible.

Research notes

Keep uncertain details editable and flag stamps that deserve a closer look.

Frequently asked questions

What should a stamp inventory contain?

A useful inventory can include a record number, photo reference, country or region, issue details where known, notes and a field for further research.

Can I catalog stamps from photos?

Yes. Clear photos can be the starting point for a structured digital inventory. The Browser Beta helps organize the records; it does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.
