Help test AI Stamp Scanner

Thank you for helping. Read the test checklist first, then try the scanner with a few stamp photos and tell us what is clear and what could be improved.

⏱ Estimated test time: 10 minutes

🎯 Test goal

Your feedback helps improve the AI Stamp Scanner Browser Beta. You do not need to be a stamp expert.

We are especially interested in:

  • Is the app easy to use?
  • Are all steps clear?
  • Was any information missing?
  • Did anything go wrong?
  • Which feature was most useful?
  • Would you use this app for your own collection?
  • Would you recommend this app to others?
  • What would you like to see added to the app?

Test checklist

  1. Read the test checklist.
  2. Upload photos. Upload one or more photos of a stamp collection.
  3. Create the stamp inventory. Check that all stamps in the photo are included.
  4. Review and edit. Change a few details in the inventory.
  5. Save as Excel.
  6. Save as CSV.
🏁 Finished? Send your feedback.
