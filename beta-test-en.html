Help test AI Stamp Scanner

Thank you for helping. Read the test checklist first, then try the scanner with a few stamp photos and tell us what is clear and what could be improved.

⏱ Estimated test time: 10 minutes

🎯 Test goal

You do not need to be a stamp expert. We want to know whether the app is easy to use, whether the steps are clear, whether information is missing, whether anything goes wrong, which feature is most useful, whether you would use it for your own collection, whether you would recommend it, and what you would like to see added.

Test checklist

  1. Read the Quick Start.
  2. Upload one or more photos of a stamp collection.
  3. Create the inventory and check that all visible stamps are included.
  4. Review and edit a few details.
  5. Save as Excel.
  6. Save as CSV.

What are we checking?

Ease of use
Can you understand what to do without technical knowledge?
Photos
Does choosing and processing photos work as expected?
Stamp inventory
Is it easy to review and correct records?
Saving
Can you save Excel and CSV files without problems?

Photo tip: photograph stamps from directly above, use good light, keep every stamp fully visible and avoid overlapping loose stamps.

Important: the Browser Beta uses photo-based detection to create an editable inventory and lets you mark records for further research. It does not determine value, rarity or authenticity.

Give feedback

Tell us what happened, what you expected and which device/browser you used. Let us know what was easy, which step was unclear, what went wrong, which feature is missing and what you would like to see added. A screenshot is very helpful when reporting an error.
