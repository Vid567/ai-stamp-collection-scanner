# Brand Guide

## Brand

- Product: **AI Stamp Collection Scanner**
- Descriptor: **AI-Assisted Inventory & Research Toolkit**
- Promise: Turn collection photos into an organized inventory and discover which stamps may need further research.

## Visual identity

- Navy `#14365E`: trust, navigation, primary actions
- Sky `#41B6E6`: AI assistance and physical-check status
- Gold `#FFD54F`: research priority
- Coral `#FF847C`: low-confidence warning
- Ink `#1A2633`: primary text
- Mist `#F6F8FB`: page background

Typography uses a modern system sans-serif stack. Headings are bold with tight tracking; body copy is calm and highly legible.

## Components

- Primary buttons: navy fill, white text, 12px radius
- Secondary buttons: white fill, subtle border
- Cards: white, 1px cool-grey border, 22px radius
- Major panels: 28–32px radius
- Shadows: soft navy, low opacity

## Voice

Clear, calm, careful and evidence-aware. Never imply certainty that the product cannot provide.
